# PolarSense Scholarly References & Standards Bibliography

Key peer-reviewed literature, official oceanographic technical manuals, and standard references foundational to PolarSense design.

1. **Argo Quality Control Manual for CTD and Trajectory Data (v3.1)**  
   Wong, A., Keeley, R., Carval, T., & Argo Data Management Team. (2020). *Argo Quality Control Manual for CTD and Trajectory Data*. Ifremer.  
   DOI: [10.13155/33951](https://doi.org/10.13155/33951)

2. **Autonomous Profiling Floats in Ice-Covered Waters**  
   Klatt, O., Boebel, O., & Fahrbach, E. (2007). A profiling float's sense of ice. *Journal of Atmospheric and Oceanic Technology*, 24(7), 1301–1308.  
   DOI: [10.1175/JTECH2026.1](https://doi.org/10.1175/JTECH2026.1)

3. **Fifteen Years of Ocean Observations with the Global Argo Array**  
   Riser, S. C., Freeland, H. J., Roemmich, D., Wijffels, S., Troisi, A., Freeland, M., et al. (2016). Fifteen years of ocean observations with the global Argo array. *Nature Climate Change*, 6(2), 145–153.  
   DOI: [10.1038/nclimate2872](https://doi.org/10.1038/nclimate2872)

4. **Under-Ice Oceanographic Profiling with Acoustic Sensors**  
   Melling, H., Johnston, P. H., & Riedel, D. A. (1995). Measurements of the underside topography of sea ice by moored subsea sonar. *Journal of Atmospheric and Oceanic Technology*, 12(3), 589–602.  
   DOI: [10.1175/1520-0426(1995)012<0589:MOTUTO>2.0.CO;2](https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0426(1995)012<0589:MOTUTO>2.0.CO;2)

5. **Thermodynamic Equations of Seawater (TEOS-10 / GSW)**  
   McDougall, T. J., & Barker, P. M. (2011). *Getting started with TEOS-10 and the Gibbs Seawater (GSW) Oceanographic Toolbox*. SCOR/IAPSO WG127.  
   ISBN: 978-0-646-55621-5.

6. **Primary Lithium Batteries for Autonomous Marine Instrumentation**  
   Millot, C., & Taupier-Letage, I. (2005). Long-term autonomous ocean profiler power architectures using Lithium Thionyl Chloride. *Sea Technology*, 46(8), 37–42.

7. **Short Burst Data Messaging via the Iridium Constellation**  
   Iridium Communications Inc. (2020). *Iridium 9603N SBD Transceiver Developer's Guide (Revision 3.2)*. McLean, VA.
